FAQ

How fast is the delivery?

We do our best to get your order to you as quickly as possible! Standard delivery usually takes between 2 to 5 business days, depending on where you’re located. If you're in a major city, it might even arrive sooner. We also offer express shipping options at checkout if you need your order in a hurry. Once your order is shipped, you'll get a tracking number so you can follow it every step of the way.

Can I buy alcohol if I’m under 18?

Unfortunately, no. By law, you must be of legal drinking age in your province or territory to buy alcohol — that’s usually 18 or 19 years old, depending on where you live. When you place an order, we may ask for ID at the time of delivery or pickup to make sure we’re staying compliant and responsible.

What if my bottle shows up broken?

We pack all our orders with care, but sometimes things happen during shipping. If your order arrives damaged or if something’s missing, just contact our customer support within 48 hours. Take a photo of the issue, and we’ll make it right — whether that means sending you a replacement, issuing a refund, or finding another solution that works for you.

Do you guys deliver everywhere?

We deliver to most cities and towns across the country, but a few remote or restricted areas may not be eligible due to alcohol shipping laws. When you check out, our system will let you know if delivery is available in your area. If not, we can help you find the nearest store or suggest other options

Can I cancel or change my order after placing it?

We get it — sometimes plans change! If you need to cancel or update your order, just reach out to us as soon as possible. If the order hasn’t been processed or shipped yet, we’ll do our best to make the changes for you. Once it's on its way, though, we might not be able to modify it — but you can still contact us to explore your options. We're always here to help, whether it's rerouting a delivery, updating an address, or making things right.F
